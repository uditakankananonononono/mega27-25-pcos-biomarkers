# PCOS - proposed separate project

Status: project scaffold and gate audit only, not a finished paper or a positive benchmark. The parent repository is shared core; this directory must hold a disease-specific protocol, accession and external-service evidence ledger, reproducible results and a 50-page substantive paper before its gates can be claimed.

Current disease-tagged manifest records: 121 = 20 GSE identifiers (including two nested SubSeries) + 100 nested GSM samples + 1 other; GSE43322 has treatment/crossover overlap risk. These are record units, not independent datasets or patients. The shared 40-service and 49-page PDF do not transfer as automatic per-project passes. Benchmark/discovery endpoint is open.

Disease-specific documents and source logs are not yet split from shared core; use the shared source code and result filenames by disease as leads, then verify original record attribution before copying.

## GSE5090 duplicate donor arrays

The old pooled discovery calls [GSE5090](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE5090) nine PCOS versus eight control adipose **arrays**, but the series description and 17 individual sample titles bind two PCOS arrays to EP1 and two control arrays to EP31: eight PCOS versus seven control **people**. We fetched every GSM and checked title, GPL96 platform, source phenotype and expression column; `sources/GSE5090_used_sample_crosswalk.csv` records source URLs and hashes. A post-audit donor-mean effect sensitivity (`scripts/pcos_gse5090_donor_sensitivity.py`) averages each duplicated EP pair before the 8-versus-7 contrast. Across 13,047 shared genes, 1,346 effect signs change and median absolute Hedges-effect shift is 0.114. This is a correction to the independent statistical unit, not a new cohort, a new PCOS marker, or a preregistered validation. The earlier pooled meta and downstream CNN/replication were not rerun and cannot be cited as donor-corrected. The 17 historically analyzed GSMs have now been entered as nested used-accession records in this standalone manifest; they represent 15 EP donor tokens, not 17 people or studies. The separate shared-repo builder branch remains unmerged here and its source ledger must be reconciled independently before any larger count.

## Used-array provenance correction (September 27)

All 17 [GSE5090](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE5090) sample full texts were freshly fetched and byte-hashed against the previously committed `GSE5090_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`. The exact seventeen GSMs equal the previously analyzed expression label set; source files are retained as lossless gzip under `sources/GSE5090/`. Each sample is associated with an EP token, and EP1 (PCOS) and EP31 (control) each have two arrays. Thus the manifest now has **71 record identifiers** rather than 54 (17 GSE, 53 nested GSM, one ontology), but GSE5090 still contributes only 15 donor tokens. Forty-nine more genuinely used records are needed for the 120 record interpretation. The post-audit donor-average effect-only sensitivity changes 1,346 of 13,047 effect signs; old pooled discovery and downstream panels have not been recomputed, so no positive validation, novel marker or benchmark advantage follows.

## P46 registered new source test, failed support (September 27)

The shared-core registration at `a5ea2bf78d4e92ad5c0a6d7257a957ff33b5475c` froze a 20-gene PCOS directional panel and matched-null rule **before** expression values from [GSE271363](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE271363) were opened. The exact registration, protocol, source metadata, input RPKM, replay script, gene effects, 10,000 null draws and result have been copied into this disease-specific repository. All 16 individual source GSMs were newly fetched and checked against the exact GEO titles and description tokens, and `sources/GSE271363_used_sample_crosswalk.csv` records URL and byte hash for each; lossless gzip source records are under `sources/p46/`. Metadata and source expression match the registered SHA-256 digests. Standalone replay with the frozen shared HGNC cache reproduces **19 of 20 genes measurable, 14 signs agreeing, matched-null mean 10.8196, empirical p=0.108189**, failing the registered p<0.025 support rule. No threshold change or favorable cohort selection follows.

This adds one genuinely analyzed parent GSE and 16 nested GSMs to the disease-specific inventory, moving 71 to **88 identifiers**: 18 GSE, 69 GSM, one ontology. It adds only one study and at most 16 source records of people; distinct GSM, title and BioSample IDs do not prove unrelated participants. The test was of an older selected panel, not a newly discovered marker, clinical classifier or fair published same-task benchmark win. The 120 record-unit interpretation remains 32 short; 40 disease-specific services and a 50-substantive-page Times paper remain open. P46's negative result remains visible.

## GSE43322 SuperSeries treatment mixture and baseline-only sensitivity (September 28)

The old [GSE43322](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE43322) `24-case versus 7-control` adipose effect actually pooled three source-defined PCOS case sets against seven baseline healthy-control arrays: eight baseline untreated PCOS cases from the cross-sectional [GSE43264](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE43264), eight placebo-exposed PCOS and eight LC n-3 PUFA-exposed PCOS from the intervention crossover [GSE43266](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE43266). It is one SuperSeries, not three independent cohorts. All 31 full GSM records were fetched/hash-checked against title, subseries, intervention, platform and all 17,126 source probes. The old effect replays from the source matrix to max absolute Hedges-g deviation 0.000052 (saved output rounding). Restricting cases to eight baseline PCOS arrays versus seven baseline controls changes the effect sign for **2,956 of 16,910** gene summaries, including 849 with |g| >= 0.2 in both contrasts; the median absolute g shift is 0.391. This is post-exposure sensitivity, **not** an untouched validation or favorable refit. The baseline and placebo labels share eight numerical suffixes, but exact donor linkage is not established by those labels; do not claim 24 independent PCOS people or assume one-to-one pairing from suffixes. The old pooled gene results remain retained but invalid for a clean untreated diagnosis claim. Source crosswalk, retained full GSMs, matrix/platform and replay outcomes are in `sources/gse43322/` and `data/geo/gse43322/`.

The 31 newly reconciled **historically used** nested GSM IDs and two source-checked SubSeries parent records move the disease inventory **88 to 121**: 20 GSE (GSE43264 and GSE43266 are both nested under already-counted GSE43322), 100 GSM and one ontology. This is one source SuperSeries and 31 arrays, not 31 independent studies or proven 31 different people. It crosses only the literal 120 source-record-unit floor, with series nesting explicitly disclosed, and does not meet the 40-services, separate fifty-page Times paper, new marker, or fair published same-task benchmark gates.
